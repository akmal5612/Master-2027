AI Detector Kin Cheezon Ko Dekhta Hai?

1. Predictability

Kya agla word bohat aasani se predict kiya ja sakta hai? AI writing mein aam tor par predictable wording hoti hai.

2. Sentence Structure

Kya har sentence ka structure ek jaisa hai, ya sentences mein natural variation hai?

3. Vocabulary Patterns

Kya paper mein baar baar ek hi academic vocabulary aur phrases use ho rahe hain?

4. Repetitive Language

Kya ek hi idea ko mukhtalif paragraphs mein repeat kiya gaya hai?

5. Writing Consistency

Kya poore paper ka writing style unusually consistent hai?

6. Sentence Length Variation

Kya tamam sentences lagbhag ek hi length ke hain, ya short aur long sentences naturally mix hain?

7. Generic Academic Language

Kya writing mein bohat zyada general academic statements hain jo kisi bhi research paper mein fit ho sakte hain?

8. Paragraph Patterns

Kya har paragraph ek hi formula follow karta hai, jaise claim, explanation aur conclusion?

9. Linguistic Patterns

Kya words aur sentences ka combination AI-generated text ke statistical patterns se milta hai?

10. Contextual Specificity

Kya research mein specific data, actual findings, relevant literature aur evidence-based discussion hai?
